Each grey dot is one of the 139,248 neurons in the FlyWire brain, seen from the front. When a note falls into a lane, that lane's visual neurons fire, then Kenyon cells, then the output neurons that choose a lane; each flashes as it spikes. For the Bio-RL fly, a hit releases reward dopamine and a mistake releases punishment dopamine, which strengthen or weaken the synapses that just fired.
